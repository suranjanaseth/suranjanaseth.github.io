Operational Framework

The Year-End Compensation Lifecycle

The chronological execution sequence I drive from initial performance reviews through to final bonus allocations:

STAGE 01
Performance Launch

Supervisory org review initiation, evaluation task reassignments, preliminary tier-load preparation, and validation for 10,000+ records.

STAGE 02
Comp Configuration

Configuring eligibility rules, bonus matrices, and Compensation Grade Profiles (CGP) aligned with Rewards COE requirements in Sandbox & Production.

STAGE 03
Joint UAT & Refreshes

Reconciling tier data flow into compensation grids, testing field editability, merit/bonus calculations, and managing manual range-warning refreshes.

STAGE 04
Live Corrections & Closures

Actioning 200+ live corrections (salary, bonus, CGP, promotion rescinds), managing liaison inbox task clearances, and statement visibility rules.

STAGE 05
APB & Post-Cycle Audits

Administering Annual Performance Bonus tickets, Rescind Business Process EIBs, and compliance audits for terminations, retirements, and sanctions.
