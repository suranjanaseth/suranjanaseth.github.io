Year-End Compensation — Configuration, EIBs & Production Readiness ▾
  • Configured and peer-reviewed Workday Advanced Compensation eligibility rules and Compensation Grade Profiles (CGP) against Rewards COE requirements; coordinated EIB extracts and loads and validated configuration changes across Sandbox and Production tenants.
  • Supported year-end bonus-plan and matrix configuration, EIB preparation, and validation; completed assigned bonus plan and matrix checks, reconciling source data with Workday across 5+ management levels and multiple performance tiers.
  • Maintained Import Employee Award (IEA) mapping files and prepared EIBs to support year-end compensation data processing, coordinating file readiness and validation with the Compensation and technical teams.
  • Loaded EIBs for mid-event CGP range changes and validated updated values against compensation reporting; supported checks of minimum, midpoint, and maximum ranges in compensation grids and the manual refresh requirement for range-warning indicators.
Compensation Event Testing & Cross-Process Validation ▾
  • Executed end-to-end year-end compensation event test scripts covering grid configuration, field display and editability, merit and bonus plan mapping, and current vs. proposed compensation data — documenting expected versus actual results and pass/fail outcomes.
  • Validated performance-tier flow into the compensation event by reconciling Workday performance data with compensation reporting and grid values during joint Compensation–Performance UAT testing.
  • Performed post-closure testing of line-of-service liaison inbox behavior, confirming compensation event tasks were no longer present after closure.
  • Tested Direct Access announcements and Career Outlook Summary visibility for assigned employee scenarios, including employees without merit increases or bonuses and those excluded from statement access.
Live-Event Corrections, Reporting & Post-Closure Support ▾
  • Processed and validated 200+ approved corrections to base salary, bonus amounts, CGP assignments, and performance tiers across lines of service; maintained supporting records and investigated discrepancies against Workday data.
  • Developed and refined year-end corrections response workbooks using Workday reports, lookup formulas, and conditional formatting; investigated employee-ID formatting and worksheet-linking issues affecting validation accuracy.
  • Scheduled compensation event reporting workflows and Alteryx reconciliation pipelines; coordinated post-load validation checks to prevent bulk bonus updates from overwriting previously validated corrections.
  • Managed Data Collection Tool (DCT) access requests, approvals, and population extracts; standardized intake processes for DCT and FTE-related event support, reducing turnaround time by approximately 40%.
  • Supported employment agreement processing and promotion rescinds, including manual bonus updates and compensation transaction rescinds for US promotion employees who had not signed required agreements.
  • Supported post-event audits by consolidating corrections forms, reviewing Workday transaction history, and investigating discrepancies with the reporting and audit team.
Year-End Performance — Launch, Closure & EIB Automation ▾
  • Managed end-to-end performance event testing and production readiness across the full lifecycle — from review initiation and supervisory-organization launches through evaluation task reassignment, tier-load preparation, and post-load validation.
  • Architected 3 Alteryx automation workflows that generated a master EIB and 9 upload files for preliminary performance-tier loads, eliminating approximately 15 hours of manual file preparation per cycle and reducing data entry errors by an estimated 80%.
  • Developed a dedicated Alteryx reconciliation workflow to cross-validate master EIB outputs against post-load Workday performance-tier reporting, ensuring data integrity for 10,000+ employee records.
  • Investigated and resolved headcount discrepancies, tier mismatches, and processing exceptions across employee populations during joint Compensation-Performance UAT testing.
  • Maintained comprehensive test scripts, project trackers, and RAID logs documenting risks, issues, decisions, and follow-up actions; contributed to SOP creation for EIB-based tier validation automation.
Annual Performance Bonus (APB) — Processing & Compliance ▾
  • Participated in cross-team knowledge transfer to support APB processing, corrections in Workday Production received via ServiceNow tickets, and compliance audits.
  • Processed APB correction requests in Workday Production, including transaction rescinds via the Rescind Business Process EIB and revised bonus amount loads based on Rewards COE inputs — handling 50+ correction tickets within SLA.
  • Built and maintained an Alteryx audit workflow to automate APB output comparison including visa suspension and sanctions cases, reducing manual audit effort by approximately 70% and improving data governance accuracy.
  • Performed comprehensive APB compliance audits to flag potential bonus adjustments related to terminations, retirement, death-in-service, and special employee groups for Rewards COE review.
BAU Compensation Operations — Configuration & Auditing ▾
